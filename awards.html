Awards & Achievements

2026

P.E.O. Scholar, Yao Ming Charitable Fund

P.E.O Scholar award is merit-based award for women of the U.S. and Canada who are pursuing a doctoral-level degree at an accredited college or university of up to $25,000. A named P.E.O. Scholar Award is a designated honor award presented to 16 top scholars.

An international scholarship of $10,000 awarded to 30 women annually who demonstrate a superior academic record conducting research applied to space sciences.
